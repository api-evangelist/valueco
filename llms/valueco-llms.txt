# ValueCo

> ValueCo (ValueCo Metrics SAS) is a Paris-based fintech that builds an objective ESG market view from the proprietary ESG scores of asset managers and institutional investors, so listed and non-listed companies can understand how investors actually perceive their ESG performance and improve their strategies. Techstars portfolio company. The platform is a web application for investors, contributors, and enterprises; ValueCo publishes no public developer portal, API documentation, or client SDKs as of 2026-07-21.

ValueCo aggregates investor-side proprietary ESG scoring into a consensus market
view ("vue de marché ESG objective"). Audiences served by the platform: asset
managers, institutional investors, listed and non-listed enterprises, and ESG
contributors. The company is based at 112 Avenue Emile Zola, 75015 Paris, France.
